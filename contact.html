Railway Helplines & Passenger Support

Essential Indian Railways official emergency helplines, official portals, and project contact information.

📞

139 - Rail Madad Helpline

Integrated All-in-One Railway Helpline for train enquiry, PNR status, medical emergency, cleanliness, and passenger assistance.

Call 139 (Toll Free)
🛡️

182 - Railway Police (RPF)

24x7 Security and Emergency Helpline for passenger safety, female passenger assistance, and theft prevention onboard trains.

Call 182 (Security)

Official National Train Enquiry System website for direct IRCTC railway network verification and train running announcements.
